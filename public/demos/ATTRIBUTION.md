### Sources and modifications

All clips: one video stream, H.264, 1280 × 720, 30 fps, yuv420p, fast-start MP4. Audio, extra streams and container metadata are removed. Source framing is retained; there is no body/technique retouching, speed change or synthetic animation. JPEG posters are still frames from one second into each adapted clip, resized to 640 × 360 (480 × 270 for the two newest clips). Durations can differ slightly after frame-rate conversion.

Reproduction command, with the interval from the table:

```sh
ffmpeg -ss START -i SOURCE -t DURATION -map 0:v:0 \
  -vf 'scale=1280:-2,fps=30' -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 26 \
  -pix_fmt yuv420p -an -sn -dn -map_metadata -1 -movflags +faststart OUTPUT.mp4
ffmpeg -ss 1 -i OUTPUT.mp4 -frames:v 1 -vf 'scale=640:-2' -q:v 4 OUTPUT.jpg
```

## Selection notes

Every movement uses real human gym footage; all earlier vector stick-figure movement animations were removed on 26 November 2026 at the user's request. Clips that show a different machine model or setup carry explicit variation notes beside the demonstration. Training prescriptions and saved history are unchanged.
